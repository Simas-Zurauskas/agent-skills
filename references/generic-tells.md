# Recognizing unconsidered defaults

Use [design-diagnostics.md](design-diagnostics.md), which governs this reference. A common visual choice is not a
failure by itself. Diagnose whether the choice serves this product and the owner's requested comparison.

| Pattern to inspect | Useful question | Possible response, only if a real issue exists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same cards and hierarchy across all variants | Did the round promise alternatives in structure? | Vary information emphasis or grouping while preserving exact content |
| Same familiar font everywhere | Is typography an intended axis, and does the face fit actual strings? | Explore another appropriate type system; no family blacklist |
| Neutral or saturated palette | Does it express the intended product tone and remain legible? | Change relationships or contrast; do not enforce saturation quotas |
| Hero, giant metric or illustrated cover | Does this screen's task warrant that emphasis? | Keep it if useful; otherwise prioritize the actual task or data |
| Flat or layered surfaces | Is grouping and hierarchy clear? | Add/remove containment where useful; no mandatory surface count |
| Lists or ordinary controls | Does familiar structure help users act and compare? | Preserve useful conventions; add character through purposeful details |
| Decorative icon tiles, rules, gradients or shadows | Do they communicate meaning or support a coherent visual idea? | Remove distraction or strengthen the intended device |
| Trend genre or vernacular | Is it translated into this product rather than pasted over content? | Ground the treatment in product behavior and real information |
| Imagery and character | What social/product meaning does it imply? | Replace misleading implications; respect explicit product constraints |
| Mockup/device framing | Does it help judge the product at its intended size? | Use appropriate framing; neither require nor ban it globally |

A round can intentionally compare quiet directions, conventional systems or one constrained family. Name where
variants differ and what decision the comparison supports. Do not add invented color/font bans or demand a novelty
quota. Grounding may come from a product ritual, existing brand, reference app or physical object; borrowing needs
provenance and a clear reason. Critic evidence must identify an actual screen, consequence and bounded repair.
