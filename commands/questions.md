# questions — useful independent scrutiny

Read [rules](../reference/rules.md), [grill](../reference/grill.md), and
[dispositions](../reference/dispositions.md). This procedure is the same review/settlement cycle used
by init, add, resolve and ready, not another agent layer after those commands.

1. Reconcile supplied decisions/owner edits, markers, unresolved contradictions, vetoes and relevant
   source drift. Open the shared write entry if standalone. Do not overwrite human answers or close
   owner rows silently. Missing old review is not presumed completed.
2. Choose risk coverage. Bare questions reviews changed outcomes and relevant neighbours; unchanged
   inputs with adequate clean coverage need no new pass unless asked. `full` explicitly requests fresh
   whole-feature scrutiny. Known owner blockers alone do not need to be re-proved by agents.
3. Dispatch the appropriate combined reviewer or builder/adversary roles. Give raw evidence and current
   context with the target scope; save actual returns. No fixed two/four grill passes, routing agent,
   cold reader or separate faithfulness agent.
4. Merge findings, verify evidence and apply scope test/settlements under dispositions. Close ordinary
   gaps as eligible defaults or corrections; serious choices become neutral question rows and markers.
   Do not promote optional safeguards into requirements or manufacture questions from engineering choices.
5. One consolidated correction batch and targeted recheck of affected outcomes/dependencies by default.
   Further material residuals leave Draft; explicit extra rounds permit further targeted work. Incomplete
   coverage stays unverified. Review changes made while settling findings, not just the original draft.
6. Regenerate Open questions, sync IDs and close per [record](../reference/record.md). Embedded callers
   share their entry/report and close only once. Record coverage/input evidence for possible ready reuse.

If the owner explicitly asks to go through questions together, show a manageable batch in impact order,
with feasible choices. Record their replies and stop when they stop; never start an uninvited interview.
