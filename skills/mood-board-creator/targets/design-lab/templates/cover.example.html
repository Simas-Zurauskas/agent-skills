How to read a variant

  1. Read the board's first line. That's the bet it makes.
  2. Find the signature. It's one idea, and it lands on a number.
  3. Squint at the screens. On Home your eye should land on the Your turn row. In the room it goes to You 1,690. On the Finish line it goes to 10,000.
  4. Read the trade-offs at the bottom of the board.

What we want from you

  • Keep, push or kill for each variant. Push means you like the idea but not this execution.
  • One thing to steal, even from a variant you'd kill.
  • The group-chat test: would you send its Finish line to four mates with "we're doing this"?
  • Across the round, pick the best Home, room and Finish line. They can come from three different variants.
