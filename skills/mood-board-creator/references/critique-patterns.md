# Evidence-led critique

Fresh reviewers follow [design-diagnostics.md](design-diagnostics.md) and receive the shared brief, owner constraints,
exact content, actual screenshots and target links. They must be independently dispatched, not the builder reviewing
its own conversation. Record actual model/route; same-model review is independent only when the dispatch is fresh.

Look for substantive failures: missing/changed content; unusable controls; clipping/overflow; incorrect images/fonts;
misleading product metaphors; a board promising an absent signature; variants that do not address the requested
comparison; and target/editability failures. Historical examples such as overlapping tap targets, absent Lucide icons,
proportional numbers in aligned columns and duplicated layout skeletons are diagnostics only where applicable.

Each finding includes variant, screen/element, screenshot or node evidence, consequence, severity and minimal repair.
Explicit owner/product constraints outrank critic taste. Familiar fonts, neutrality, lists, a missing hero or a low
surface count do not establish a defect. Preference-only alternatives go in optional notes. No score rubric or
minimum number of findings is required. An empty substantive finding list is a valid result.

The round critic reviews all boards and full-size screens plus matching-screen comparisons. Normal uses
that one fresh build-stage critique; thorough adds per-variant critics. Critics write notes and never publish shared
pages or mutate canvas nodes. If screenshots or a required variant are missing, mark review incomplete.

Only affected variants receive a repair dispatch. Refiner applies accepted substantive fixes, preserves agreed content
and direction, captures touched output and reports remaining issues. The lead verifies the changed output and any
shared comparison affected. One bounded repair pass; unresolved substantive issues retain incomplete status, not an
infinite polishing loop. Self-review does not substitute for a failed required independent reviewer.
